How to use this calculator

  • Measure power at the film if you can. The laser's label rating is not necessarily the power that reaches the recording material.
  • Estimate the illuminated area. A 10 × 10 cm sheet has an area of 100 cm², but the beam may illuminate only part of it.
  • Choose a target dose based on the photopolymer manufacturer's documentation and refine it with test exposures.
  • Keep the setup still. Vibration during exposure can destroy the fine interference pattern.
